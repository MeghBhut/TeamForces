TeamForces — House Rules

Welcome! This is our private contest tracker. A few ground rules:

During a contest

  • Solve the problems on Codeforces using your registered handle — solves are detected automatically.
  • No sharing solutions, hints, or discussing problems until the contest ends.
  • The results table shows solve times and wrong attempts, but there are no ranks and no leaderboard. Compete with your past self.

After a contest

  • Problems you didn't finish go to your Upsolve queue — solve them later and they'll be marked done.
  • Write a mistake log for problems that tripped you up. Everyone reads these, so be honest — it helps the whole group.

Privacy

  • Contest results and mistake logs are shared with the group.
  • Your stats and bookmarks are private — only you can see them.

Admins: edit web/rules.html to change this page.
